One phone test door. Grok stages the candidate directly from Test; you tap once to run it. No normal ZIP handoff or Downloads hunt.

Normal workflow. Grok builds/stages directly in the Test repository. The Player reads one tiny latest-build pointer, then opens that exact uniquely staged build. You do not download or re-upload the candidate.

Emergency/manual fallback only. Choose a candidate ZIP directly from Downloads. Old ZIP-preview state is purged before another can load.

Authoritative check: https://jeromeleegriffin.github.io/Test/

Rule: Grok creates/stages exactly one candidate directly from the clean Test baseline under a unique /Test/builds/<id>/ path and updates /Test/player/latest.json. The Player never guesses a build and never falls back to an older one. Production is never opened.
