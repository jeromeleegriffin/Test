GRIFFIN HOUSE OF ROOKS
SCENE-INTEGRATED SEATED PLAYER — MASTER CONSTRUCTION AUTHORITY
Date: 2026-10-05
Status: APPROVED / BANKED DESIGN & CONSTRUCTION AUTHORITY

PURPOSE
This file preserves the complete lessons learned during the TABLE-003 seated-player/avatar visual study so future avatar construction can begin from the proven solution instead of rediscovering it.

AUTHORITY SPLIT — CRITICAL
1. The approved seated-player reference image is authority for PLAYER CONSTRUCTION, SEATING ILLUSION, OCCLUSION, DEPTH, SCALE RELATIONSHIPS, and CHARACTER/TABLE INTERACTION.
2. It is NOT pixel authority for cards, card counts/values, gameplay state, UI, Bid Box, room details, or exact table geometry. Generative interpretation occurred in those areas.
3. The actual real-runtime TABLE-003 Final Calibration Candidate remains authority for actual game pixels/UI/table runtime geometry.
4. The TABLE003 Master Table Design Authority remains the physical environment/table-design reference where applicable.
5. If authorities conflict: real runtime wins for game/UI; locked TABLE-003 wins for table; this file + approved seated-player image win for how players are physically integrated.

CORE VISUAL GOAL
Cinder, Dagger and Crow must read as REAL PEOPLE PHYSICALLY SEATED AROUND THE TABLE, not avatars pasted over a game. No visible chairs are needed; pose, perspective, rail occlusion, and arm contact must make the viewer infer seated bodies/chairs.

ONLY THREE SCENE-INTEGRATED CHARACTERS
- Cinder: top/far seat.
- Dagger: left seat.
- Crow: right seat.
- NO bottom/Jerome avatar or body. Human player is first-person camera/hand.

PROVEN DEPTH / LAYER MODEL
The earlier failures came from treating a character as one flat portrait layer. Do not do that.
Conceptual stack:
L0 room/background
L1 seated character torsos/shoulders/body mass
L2 AUTHORITATIVE physical table rail/apron OCCLUSION MASK
L3 only deliberately exposed foreground hand/forearm resting on top of rail
L4 felt/play surface
L5 legitimate played cards/game objects
L6 necessary game UI

MASTER RULE
THE TABLE MUST BLOCK THE PEOPLE. THE TABLE MUST NOT BLOCK THE GAME.

AUTHORITATIVE RAIL OCCLUSION
- The existing wooden rail is a HARD PHYSICAL OCCLUDER for character bodies.
- The inner/top contour of the rail is a hard clipping boundary.
- Torso, chest, lower shoulder, clothing, waist, background arm portions, hair, etc. have zero permission to leak through the rail onto felt.
- No feathering, transparency, blending, ghost pixels, or artistic interpretation across the rail.
- If body geometry conflicts with the rail, hide/crop/reposition the body. The table wins.
- Felt is gameplay space, not character space.

FOREGROUND LIMB EXCEPTION
A hand/forearm may intentionally come forward and rest ON TOP OF the wooden rail. This must be a separate, physically believable foreground interaction: body remains behind rail; limb approaches and rests on rail top. It must not emerge through wood or accidentally intrude onto felt. Crow's approved treatment is the model for this.

CINDER
- Far/top seat; frontal toward human player.
- Naturally somewhat smaller because farther from camera.
- Head + shoulders + upper chest + only enough torso to imply seated body.
- Lower torso disappears behind far rail.
- Never floats above table, sits on table, or penetrates felt.
- Seat geometry determines placement, NOT fireplace centerline.
- Fireplace remains shifted right in TABLE-003.

DAGGER
- Left seat.
- This was the repeated failure point during experimentation.
- Torso/chest/lower shoulder/clothing/waist/background arm portions must remain COMPLETELY behind left rail.
- Inner edge of left rail is absolute clipping boundary.
- Zero torso/clothing/body pixels inside felt.
- If arm comes forward, it must visibly rest ON TOP of wood; otherwise keep it behind rail.
- Better to hide more body than violate the table boundary.
- Approved final reference successfully demonstrates the correct solution.

CROW
- Right seat.
- Torso remains behind right rail.
- Approved reference demonstrates successful natural forearm/hand interaction on top of rail.
- Preserve this physical logic: torso behind; deliberate foreground limb on wood.
- No torso extension onto felt.

PERSPECTIVE / SCALE
- Do not use identical character scale.
- Cinder farthest/slightly smaller.
- Dagger/Crow side seats perceptually closer but must not become giant foreground figures.
- Table remains dominant physical object.
- Character crop: head + shoulders + upper chest/small torso. Not full bodies, not standing waist-up portraits, not floating heads, not tiny profile icons.

BORDERLESS PRESENTATION
- No circle, oval, rectangle, shield, medallion, ornate avatar frame, or visible avatar container.
- Natural alpha silhouette is the avatar boundary.
- Remove all old circular avatar regions and ghost remnants completely.
- No faded duplicate faces or stale avatar backgrounds.

LIGHT / SEPARATION
- Characters belong to the room lighting.
- Restrained warm environmental highlights where physically appropriate.
- No generic gold halo, sparks, magical glow, or neon outline.
- If separation is needed, use a very restrained adaptive dark edge, perceptually ~1–2 phone pixels, charcoal/brown-black, only where needed.
- Tight soft contact/depth shadow only as support. Rail occlusion is the primary depth cue.

IDENTITY LABELS
- Preserve Cinder, Dagger, Crow names with restrained compact identity treatment.
- No giant plates, invented levels, or unrelated badges.
- Game-state labels such as BID 100 / PASS seen in generated reference are NOT permanent avatar design authority; they are gameplay-state/UI placeholders.

YOUR PARTNER
- Visible 'YOUR PARTNER' presentation is approved for removal on next authorized roll.
- Remove presentation only; do not alter partner gameplay/logic.
- Leave no placeholder/gap/ghost text.

LOCKED / PROTECTED AREAS
- Characters adapt to TABLE-003; table does not adapt to characters.
- Do not change table perspective/taper/curvature/length/width/rails/front wood/apron/felt to fit players.
- Bid Box / Bid Dock remain protected and untouched.
- Do not invent cards, card suits, hands, scoreboards, controls, or gameplay.
- Griffin House of Rooks uses its actual card system; no poker hearts/diamonds/clubs/spades.
- No chairs.
- No fourth/bottom player.

PIXEL AUTHORITY WARNING
The approved generated seated-player image is NOT a literal implementation screenshot. It successfully proves the construction concept but generatively altered some game details (including hand/card details and some character/costume/UI interpretation). Never copy those generated gameplay pixels into production merely because they appear in the reference.

IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY WHEN AVATAR BUILDING BEGINS
1. Retrieve and read this exact Master Brain file BEFORE writing implementation instructions.
2. Retrieve/view the approved seated-player master reference image.
3. Retrieve/view the actual real-runtime TABLE-003 Final Calibration Candidate for pixel/runtime authority.
4. Audit current DOM/rendering, avatar wrappers, transforms, z-index, clipping/masking, overflow, pseudo-elements, and runtime inline styles before changes.
5. Implement deliberate body-behind-rail masking/occlusion structurally; do not rely on lucky positioning.
6. If a foreground arm is used, treat it as an intentional separated foreground limb layer constrained to rail contact.
7. Preserve underlying game/table geometry and protected systems.
8. Verify on actual phone at normal viewing distance AND pixel-peep all three rail/body intersections.
9. Explicitly inspect Dagger first because that intersection repeatedly failed in visual experiments.
10. Verify no old circle/ghost remnants and no body pixels leak onto felt.
11. Failed-fix rule applies: REMOVE / RESTORE / REDIAGNOSE / REPLACE. Do not layer patches.
