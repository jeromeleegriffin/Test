# Landscape holder and card repair — isolated owner review

Open index.html or load the ZIP in the separate GriffinHouseZipReview.html. Count controls: 0, 1, 3, 6, 10. Full screen is available. This is a visual placement prototype, not a playable game or live deployment.

The side holders were authored IN THE EXACT SCENE CAMERA, then extracted as registered transparent PNGs. There is no rotated standalone product image, stretched wood strip or CSS-drawn physical holder. Both sides use the same small dark walnut/brass design with tight contact shading. Existing approved card images are composited on their player side; top edges displace toward table center, away from seated owners. Transparent object artwork occludes the card bases.

The woman's cards now draw BEHIND the entire existing front/support artwork, not over its exterior wooden face. The bottom cards insert 9px below the retaining lip. Card counts do not move the wooden holders. The original approved scene image is the fixed base; generated image pixels are restricted by masks to the two local side-holder regions. The real approved lantern SVG remains an independent layer. Walnut is retained as a future material concept; pose changes remain banked.

## Rejected methods
Previous rotated product-view holders floated and had incorrect perspective/lean. Stretched backrest strips and repeated math patches are rejected. An initial generous extraction mask hid lower card pixels with blue felt, creating an apparent gap; it was corrected BEFORE this package. Old top layering exposed card bottoms across the exterior wood; replaced with full registered foreground occlusion.
